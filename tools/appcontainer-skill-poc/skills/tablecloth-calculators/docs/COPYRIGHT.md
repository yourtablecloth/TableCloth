# Copyright boundaries for independent calculations

[한국어](COPYRIGHT.ko.md) | [README](../README.md) | [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

As of October 25, 2026, this project implements public financial and everyday calculation methods with original code and documentation. It does not import calculator-site code, screens, explanations, reports or datasets. This policy sets contribution boundaries; it does not determine the legality of a particular implementation.

## Methods and concrete expression

Korea's Copyright Act defines copyright works as creative expression and distinguishes a program's languages, conventions and algorithms from its protected expression. Article 101-2 describes the excluded algorithm as the method of combining instructions and commands. A public calculation method therefore differs from someone else's concrete source code, wording, graphics or presentation. This distinction does not authorize copying those materials. See [Article 2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0002&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) and [Article 101-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=02&joNo=0101&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR).

Contributions can cite a public formula or primary specification and independently derive code, explanations and tests. Copying, translating, paraphrasing or rewriting a third-party implementation or its accompanying content into this package is outside the accepted scope.

## Datasets and collections

A numerical method does not grant reuse rights over tables, reference populations, content collections or a service's database. Article 93 covers reproduction and distribution of all or a substantial part of a protected database and addresses certain repeated or systematic uses of smaller portions. Article 2 separately defines creative selection, arrangement and structure. See [Article 93](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0093&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) and [Article 2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0002&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR).

This package operates on explicit inputs and includes no extracted site datasets. Basic BMI arithmetic does not include population percentiles or medical classifications. Holiday, lunar-calendar, current-price and named-zone DST datasets remain outside scope.

## Site-specific services and PRO labels

Do not reproduce third-party user interfaces, help text, report formats, proprietary scores, datasets or site-specific PRO workflows. A PRO label, public availability or a claim of copyright alone does not establish exclusive legal rights over an underlying general calculation method. Nor does the absence of such a label grant reuse permission.

Independent implementation of public methods sets the project boundary. It does not provide a blanket assurance against patent, trademark, contract, unfair-competition or other claims. No specific service's exclusive rights or a particular contribution's legal clearance is asserted here. [Article 101-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=02&joNo=0101&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR) does not resolve those separate questions.

## Provenance, review and project licenses

Each contribution records its authorship, public-method references, included material's license and usage terms, and independent derivation as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Maintainers review scope and unresolved rights before inclusion. Attribution does not replace permission or make excluded material acceptable.

The package retains TableCloth's [AGPL license text](../LICENSE-AGPL) and [commercial-license contact notice](../LICENSE-COMMERCIAL) unchanged, including its original attribution. These files govern the project's licensing notices; they do not grant rights in third-party material.
